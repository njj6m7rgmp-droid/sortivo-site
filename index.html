Sortivo

Sort your photos with one tap.

Sortivo shows one photo at a time. Tap a button and the photo goes to the album you chose in Apple Photos, or is copied to a folder in your own OneDrive or Google Drive. Nothing is deleted unless you decide so, and your original photos always stay in your Photos library.

  • One-tap buttons with your own folder names and colors
  • Albums in Photos, a delete-review folder, OneDrive and Google Drive
  • Background uploads with progress on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • No account with us, no servers, no tracking
